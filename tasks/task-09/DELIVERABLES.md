- `src/job.py`: deliberately unreliable baseline.
- `src/reference_solution.py`: independent passing oracle.
- `data/`: deterministic normal and conflicting inputs.

- `run-tests.sh` and `solution.sh`: baseline and oracle runners.
- `REQUIREMENT_MATRIX.md`, `MUTATION_TESTS.md`, `VALIDATION_REPORT.md`, `ROLLOUT_RECORD.md`, `QA_REPORT.md`, and `LIMITATIONS.md`: evidence and review artifacts.
